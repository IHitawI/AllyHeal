# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-17

First release.

- Heal, Great Heal, Great Heal Excerpt and Replenishment also heal allies within 3 m for 75%.
- Magic Barrier, Great Magic Barrier, Escape Death, Flash Sweat, Remedy, Resist Curse, Hidden Body,
  Hush, Fall Control, Cast Light and Repair also apply to allies within 3 m at full strength.
- Allies only: enemies and PvP opponents are never affected.
- Built on vanilla parameters.
